# Evrosion R-001 — Economic Independence & Sustainable Open-Source Model

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Series:** R — Release, Project & Sustainability  
**Decision:** R-001  
**Purpose:** Define how Evrosion remains genuinely open source, financially sustainable, difficult to capture, and capable of surviving competition from much larger platform companies without betraying its ownership principles.

---

# 1. Core Principle

> **Evrosion monetizes value around the operating system, never control over the owner.**

The Evrosion core operating system is intended to remain genuinely open source.

The project must not depend on:

- forced accounts;
- advertising;
- mandatory telemetry;
- artificial feature crippling;
- paid access to essential security fixes;
- removal of owner control;
- proprietary lock-in to basic OS functionality.

Evrosion should be capable of becoming financially powerful without turning the operating system itself into a mechanism for extracting rent from the person who owns the computer.

---

# 2. Economic Goal

Evrosion must be designed to survive even if it eventually competes with companies many orders of magnitude larger than the project was at launch.

The economic objective is therefore not merely:

> "make enough money to keep the servers running."

It is:

> **build enough independent economic strength that Evrosion cannot easily be starved, bought, pressured, or outspent into abandoning its principles.**

This includes:

- sustainable recurring revenue;
- diversified income;
- reserves;
- paid maintainers;
- security funding;
- legal-defense capacity;
- OEM and enterprise relationships;
- a project structure that can survive temporary commercial failure.

---

# 3. Open Source Is Part of the Defense

Open source is not treated only as a monetization constraint.

It is also a resilience mechanism.

Once Evrosion source code, architecture, history, releases, and build processes are publicly available and mirrored:

- one company cannot erase the project;
- one acquisition cannot erase prior open releases;
- one infrastructure provider cannot permanently remove access;
- one commercial failure does not necessarily kill the codebase;
- the community can preserve or fork the project if stewardship ever fails.

Evrosion-the-business and Evrosion-the-project should reinforce one another without being identical in purpose.

---

# 4. The Core OS Remains Free

Ordinary users should be able to:

- obtain Evrosion;
- install it;
- use it;
- inspect the source;
- modify it;
- build it;
- redistribute it under the applicable open-source license;
- receive essential security fixes during supported lifetimes;

without paying a mandatory OS license fee.

The right to own and control the machine must not depend on a subscription.

---

# 5. Security Updates Remain Free

Essential security fixes for supported Evrosion releases must not be placed behind a paid plan.

Commercial offerings may provide:

- extended support;
- guaranteed response times;
- enterprise tooling;
- certification;
- migration assistance;
- managed services;
- compliance services;
- hosted infrastructure;

but a vulnerability fix required to keep a supported Evrosion installation secure must not become a paywall mechanism.

---

# 6. Revenue Must Come From Multiple Independent Sources

Evrosion should deliberately avoid dependence on a single revenue stream.

Candidate long-term revenue sources include:

1. **Voluntary sponsorship and donations**
2. **Grants and institutional funding**
3. **Professional engineering and contract development**
4. **Enterprise support and lifecycle services**
5. **Hardware certification**
6. **OEM integration and support agreements**
7. **Official OEM branding and distribution services**
8. **Marketplace/platform services**
9. **Optional hosted/cloud services**
10. **Optional AI services and credits**
11. **Developer infrastructure/services**
12. **Commercial licensing arrangements where appropriate**
13. **Training, certification, migration, and consulting**
14. **Future certified hardware or reference-device programs**

No individual source should become so dominant that losing one customer, sponsor, OEM, or service threatens the project's existence.

The exact acceptable concentration thresholds are a later financial-governance decision.

---

# 7. OEM Revenue Model

Evrosion shall not rely on a mandatory open-source software royalty merely because an OEM installs the public Evrosion code.

OEM revenue instead comes from commercial value surrounding the official relationship.

Possible OEM services include:

- Evrosion hardware certification;
- factory image engineering;
- firmware integration;
- driver/hardware enablement;
- recovery integration;
- signed official images;
- release coordination;
- update validation;
- long-term compatibility testing;
- engineering escalation;
- support SLAs;
- official Evrosion branding/trademark rights;
- lifecycle coordination;
- OEM-specific tooling;
- priority certification and regression testing.

Commercial OEM agreements may use:

- per-device fees;
- per-model certification fees;
- fixed engineering fees;
- annual contracts;
- support subscriptions;
- combinations of the above.

Exact pricing is not part of R-001 and must be determined by economics and negotiation at the time.

---

# 8. Evrosion Certified and Layer 3

Evrosion Certified covers a defined supported configuration.

Layer 3 remains fully functional, but owner modification moves the system outside the Evrosion Certified software envelope.

This allows:

- owner sovereignty;
- OEM support boundaries;
- trustworthy support diagnostics;
- restoration back to a certified configuration.

Leaving certification must never revoke ownership rights.

Returning to the canonical verified configuration should allow certification to be restored where the hardware remains valid.

The detailed mechanism is defined separately in the Evrosion Certified privacy/diagnostic and platform-sovereignty work.

---

# 9. No Mandatory OEM Telemetry

OEM partnerships must not override Evrosion's privacy principles.

Evrosion Certified systems must not require:

- hidden background telemetry;
- mandatory usage analytics;
- mandatory reliability reporting;
- advertising tracking;
- silent OEM data channels.

Local observability is permitted and encouraged.

Support data may be transmitted through explicit, owner-authorized, inspectable diagnostic workflows.

Optional reliability programs may exist, but they must remain optional and transparent.

Commercial money does not buy an exception to this rule.

---

# 10. Marketplace Revenue

Evrosion may operate an official software marketplace.

The marketplace may charge a platform fee for commercial transactions.

The project's direction is to keep this fee materially lower than dominant incumbent platform stores where practical, while still covering:

- payment processing;
- fraud prevention;
- infrastructure;
- moderation;
- malware/security review;
- package signing/distribution;
- developer services;
- continued platform development.

The previously discussed **10%** fee remains a target/working concept, not a permanent constitutional number.

The marketplace must not become mandatory for installing software.

Evrosion's native software model must continue to allow software distribution outside the official store, subject to the user's responsibility layer and security choices.

---

# 11. Optional AI Revenue

Evrosion may provide paid optional cloud-AI services.

Possible models include:

- usage credits;
- monthly allocations;
- provider pass-through;
- premium model access;
- hosted local-to-cloud orchestration.

AI remains optional.

A user who disables or refuses cloud AI must still have a complete operating system.

Cloud AI must not become a hidden dependency for:

- boot;
- recovery;
- file access;
- security;
- basic configuration;
- essential system management.

AI revenue must remain subordinate to E-021:

> **AI may decide what to propose. AI never decides what authority it has.**

---

# 12. Enterprise Revenue

Organizations may use the open-source Evrosion system without buying an ordinary user license.

Evrosion may sell enterprise products and services around that deployment, including:

- support SLAs;
- fleet management;
- deployment tooling;
- controlled update channels;
- lifecycle guarantees;
- security response;
- direct engineering escalation;
- compliance documentation;
- migration services;
- deployment consulting;
- managed infrastructure.

Organizations pay for operational certainty, support, integration, accountability, and services.

They do not purchase extra sovereignty over ordinary users or architectural authority over Evrosion itself.

---

# 13. Contributors Are Not Automatically Paid Per Contribution

Normal open-source contributions are voluntary unless otherwise agreed.

Evrosion should not create automatic payment-per-commit, payment-per-line, or perpetual revenue-sharing obligations for ordinary contributions.

Those mechanisms create poor incentives and are operationally difficult to define fairly.

Instead, Evrosion may fund work through:

- paid contracts;
- predefined bounties;
- funded issues;
- security bounties;
- maintainer stipends;
- grants;
- employment;
- sponsorship.

---

# 14. Contributor / Maintainer Fund

When commercial revenue becomes meaningful, Evrosion should establish a dedicated mechanism for funding important open development.

Potential uses include:

- key maintainer stipends;
- security work;
- difficult driver development;
- accessibility engineering;
- long-term maintenance;
- documentation;
- testing;
- architecture validation;
- critical infrastructure;
- funded roadmap work.

The exact funding formula is not locked by R-001.

The important rule is:

> **Commercial success should strengthen the open project that created it.**

---

# 15. Contributor Rights

The preferred direction is that contributors retain ownership/copyright in their original contributions while granting Evrosion the durable rights required to:

- use;
- modify;
- distribute;
- sublicense where necessary;
- include contributions in official releases;
- operate compatible commercial licensing arrangements where legally structured to do so.

The exact Contributor Agreement, CLA/DCO model, relicensing rights, and public license choice are deferred to the licensing/governance decision.

R-001 does **not** require contributors to surrender ownership of their work.

---

# 16. Survival Reserve

Once Evrosion becomes meaningfully profitable, it should establish a dedicated survival reserve.

Its purpose is to keep core project operations functioning through:

- loss of a major OEM;
- recession;
- revenue collapse;
- market pressure;
- litigation;
- infrastructure disruption;
- competitive price pressure;
- temporary commercial failure.

A mature target may eventually be expressed as a number of months of core operating expenditure.

A conceptual target of roughly **18–24 months** has been discussed, but R-001 does not freeze that figure.

The reserve is strategic survival capital, not routine growth spending.

---

# 17. Legal-Defense Capacity

If Evrosion reaches meaningful scale, commercial success must fund legal resilience.

This may include:

- trademark protection;
- patent-defense participation;
- defensive publication;
- contribution provenance records;
- licensing enforcement where necessary;
- security incident counsel;
- regulatory/compliance support;
- litigation reserve;
- specialist IP counsel.

The project should not wait until it is already under pressure to establish basic ownership and provenance records.

---

# 18. Investment Principle

External investment is not forbidden.

However:

> **Evrosion should avoid becoming dependent on investment for survival.**

Investment should ideally act as an accelerator, not oxygen.

Early funding that grants disproportionate control over architecture, ownership principles, privacy, or Layer 3 should be treated as strategically dangerous.

Future capital structures are a separate governance decision.

---

# 19. Funding Cannot Purchase Architectural Authority

This is a core R-001 invariant.

> **Funding purchases services, contracts, products, support, integration, or economic participation. It does not purchase Evrosion's constitutional principles.**

Examples:

An OEM may pay for:

- hardware enablement;
- certification;
- priority engineering;
- support;
- distribution;
- branding.

It may **not** purchase the right to require:

- mandatory telemetry;
- removal of Layer 3;
- forced accounts;
- advertising integration;
- permanent owner lockout;
- hidden privileged OEM control.

A sponsor may fund development.

It does not own technical truth.

A customer may purchase engineering.

It does not receive architectural veto power merely because it pays more.

---

# 20. Revenue Concentration Is a Strategic Risk

Evrosion should explicitly monitor financial dependency.

If any single:

- OEM;
- investor;
- enterprise customer;
- marketplace;
- cloud provider;
- sponsor;
- AI provider;
- government grant;

becomes capable of threatening Evrosion by withdrawing funding, the project has acquired a strategic weakness.

Diversification should therefore be treated as infrastructure rather than merely financial optimization.

---

# 21. First Public Releases Are Credibility Releases

Evrosion 0.1 is not expected to maximize revenue.

Its primary commercial value is proving that the architecture works.

The 0.1 success path remains centered on:

```text
ev run
→ UEFI/QEMU boot
→ kernel
→ Root Supervisor
→ isolated userspace services
→ capability IPC
→ persistent storage
→ graphical shell
→ input
→ Hello.zion
→ trusted file picker
→ scoped file grant
→ deliberate app crash
→ shell survives
→ structured crash record
→ app restarts
→ deliberately broken System Generation
→ rollback to Known Good
```

Early releases primarily buy:

- credibility;
- contributors;
- technical validation;
- community;
- visibility;
- future commercial leverage.

The project should not cripple early Evrosion merely to manufacture revenue before it has earned strategic value.

A likely economic progression is:

Revenue sources:

- personal funding;
- donations;
- sponsorship;
- grants;
- small contracts.

Primary goal:

> Prove Evrosion exists and works.

## Stage 2 — Community

Revenue sources expand to:

- sponsorship;
- grants;
- funded development;
- consulting;
- early support;
- contributor funding.

Primary goal:

---

## Stage 3 — Product

Revenue expands to:

- commercial support;
- professional services;
- hardware certification;
- OEM engineering;
- hosted services;
- optional AI;
- developer services.

Primary goal:

> Make Evrosion financially self-sustaining.

---

Revenue expands to:

- major OEM agreements;
- enterprise contracts;
- marketplace;
- large-scale hosted services;
- certification ecosystem;
- broader commercial licensing.

Primary goal:

> Build economic scale without compromising ownership.

---

Evrosion maintains:

- diversified recurring revenue;

- contributor funding;
- survival reserve;
- legal-defense capacity;

- multiple OEM/enterprise relationships.

Primary goal:

> Become difficult to financially kill or capture.

- Contributor License Agreement wording;
- DCO requirements;
- legal entity structure;
- company vs foundation vs hybrid;
- founder-control mechanism;
- trademark ownership entity;
- exact OEM prices;
- exact marketplace fee;
- exact supporter subscription pricing;
- exact contributor-fund percentage;
- exact reserve size;
- exact investment structure;
- exact enterprise pricing.

These are implementation/governance questions under later R-decisions.

R-001 establishes the economic laws they must obey.

---

# 24. Locked R-001 Laws

The following are the accepted R-001 direction:

1. **Evrosion's core remains genuinely open source.**
2. **Ordinary ownership/control of the OS must not require payment.**
3. **Essential security fixes remain free during supported lifetimes.**
4. **No ads, forced accounts, mandatory telemetry, or artificial OS crippling as a revenue model.**
5. **Evrosion monetizes services, integration, support, commerce, certification, and optional services around the OS.**
6. **OEMs may pay through certification, integration, support, branding, engineering, per-device agreements, or other commercial contracts — not because the public source code itself is held hostage.**
7. **Revenue should be diversified.**
8. **Commercial success should fund open development and maintainers.**
9. **A survival reserve and legal-defense capability should be built when revenue permits.**
10. **Funding does not purchase architectural authority.**
11. **The open project must remain capable of surviving commercial setbacks.**
12. **Investment is optional and should be an accelerator rather than a dependency.**
13. **Early releases prioritize credibility and technical proof over extraction of revenue.**
14. **The economic model must remain compatible with Layer 3, owner sovereignty, privacy, offline capability, and the rest of the Evrosion architectural constitution.**

---

# 25. Final Principle

> **Evrosion should become economically powerful because people and organizations choose to pay for the value built around it — not because the operating system takes control away from the person who owns the computer.**

That principle is the foundation of Evrosion's commercial strategy.
