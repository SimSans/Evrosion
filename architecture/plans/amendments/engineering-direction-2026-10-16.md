# Engineering direction

Public technical edition, based on the 2026-10-16 engineering direction.
The source map records this edition's actual identity.

Evrosion remains an easier, self-configuring, owner-controlled, recoverable and
expandable operating system that preserves the computer's full power. Its
capability, data-preservation, offline, compatibility and accessibility guarantees
must not disappear through implementation convenience.

Routine implementation improvements are appropriate when they serve the design.
Significant mechanism refinements require evidence, documented risks and tests
while preserving the guarantees. Foundational or product/legal guarantee changes
require an explicit accepted decision through the governance process.

Measure weak mechanisms, compare alternatives and prefer clear ownership,
bounded resources, failure containment and recoverable durable state. Newness or
conceptual elegance alone is not evidence of improvement. Record uncertainty and
preserve failed experiments and their actual inputs.

Read the governing architecture, accepted amendments, contracts and ADRs before
changing their boundaries. Derived summaries do not outrank their sources. Fix
code, make a supported ADR or record a narrow exception when implementation and
architecture disagree. Do not restart settled planning without an actual conflict.

Continue through the current milestone's executable gates. Keep implementation,
model tests, actual execution and future requirements distinct. Working code does
not authorize a release, grant ambient authority or silently erase user data.
