Evrosion — Business & Ecosystem Strategy


Status: STRATEGIC DIRECTION — DECIDED
Exact legal/licensing implementation: TBD

B-001 — Open Core, No Coercive Monetization

Evrosion's core operating system should remain open and usable without forcing users into subscriptions, advertisements, telemetry, accounts, cloud services, or artificial feature restrictions.

Evrosion should never make the user's computer worse simply because they are not paying.

Principle:

«Users pay for optional value, services, early access, or commercial rights — not for basic ownership or security of their computer.»

---

B-002 — Lifetime Security Updates

Security updates for supported Evrosion installations should remain free for life.

Security should never become a subscription hostage mechanism.

Free security updates include, where applicable:

- Security patches
- Critical vulnerability fixes
- Security-related bug fixes
- Essential compatibility/security maintenance
- Emergency security responses

Optional paid services must never be required simply to keep a supported computer secure.

---

B-003 — Commercial Licensing

Evrosion should support commercial licensing for organizations that want to build businesses around the platform.

Potential licensing arrangements may allow companies to:

- Build commercial products based on Evrosion
- Make proprietary modifications where permitted by the applicable license
- Keep certain modifications/code private
- Receive commercial support
- Obtain deployment or enterprise tooling
- Receive additional contractual services

The exact open-source and commercial licensing structure will be designed later with appropriate legal advice.

Important: The licensing model must support Evrosion's long-term survival without undermining its ownership principles.

---

B-004 — Optional Preview/Beta Subscriptions

Evrosion may offer optional paid subscriptions for users and organizations who want access to development builds and early functionality.

Potential subscription benefits:

- Beta patches
- Experimental features
- Preview releases
- Development channels
- Early access to new functionality
- Developer previews
- Additional testing/debugging tools where appropriate

These subscriptions are optional.

Stable users should not need a subscription to receive normal security and supported-system maintenance.

---

B-005 — Evrosion Marketplace

Evrosion should have its own optional software marketplace.

The marketplace should be designed to be attractive to developers through fair economics rather than forced platform lock-in.

Proposed marketplace principles

- Target platform fee: 10%
- Developer receives approximately 90% before applicable payment processing, taxes, refunds, and other unavoidable costs
- Developers may distribute their software outside the Evrosion Marketplace
- Developers should not be forced to use the Evrosion Marketplace
- Developers should be able to sell directly through their own websites where technically and legally feasible
- Open-source software is welcome
- Free software is welcome
- Paid software is welcome
- Subscriptions are welcome
- Donations/tips may be supported
- Automatic updates should be supported
- Cryptographic signing should be supported
- Software provenance should be visible
- Permission requirements should be transparent
- Security/malware checks should protect users without becoming unnecessary censorship
- Marketplace ranking/discovery should be transparent and designed to avoid manipulative practices

Core principle

«Developers should choose Evrosion because it treats them well, not because Evrosion prevents them from leaving.»

The 10% figure is currently a commercial target, not an immutable technical/foundational rule. Final pricing must account for payment processing, fraud, refunds, taxes/VAT, hosting, bandwidth, infrastructure, support, and applicable regulations.

---

B-006 — OEM & Preinstallation Strategy

Evrosion should be inexpensive and attractive for hardware manufacturers to preinstall.

Potential OEM value:

- Low licensing cost
- Easy hardware certification
- Automatic hardware configuration
- Strong recovery system
- Excellent out-of-box experience
- Long-term support
- No mandatory advertising model
- No requirement to compromise user ownership

OEM licensing should provide revenue without turning the consumer experience into an advertising platform.

---

B-007 — Optional Cloud & AI Services

Cloud and AI can provide additional revenue without becoming fundamental dependencies.

Potential services include:

- Hosted AI
- AI credits
- Cloud backup
- Synchronization
- Remote services
- Enterprise infrastructure
- Collaboration services
- Additional compute

These services should remain optional.

Evrosion itself must remain useful without them.

---

B-008 — Enterprise & Professional Services

Additional revenue may come from organizations that need professional infrastructure and support.

Potential offerings:

- Enterprise support
- Deployment tooling
- Fleet management
- Commercial support contracts
- Professional diagnostics
- Certification programs
- Custom integrations
- Hardware/vendor partnerships
- Long-term enterprise maintenance
- Specialized development services

---

B-009 — Ecosystem Philosophy

Evrosion should not depend on users being trapped inside its ecosystem.

A healthy ecosystem should emerge because:

1. The OS is excellent.
2. Developers are treated fairly.
3. Users retain ownership and freedom.
4. Distribution is easy.
5. Security is strong.
6. The platform provides genuine value.
7. Commercial services provide useful advantages rather than artificial restrictions.

Guiding principle

«Make people want to use Evrosion. Never make them unable to leave it.»

Evrosion's potential revenue sources:

«Evrosion should make money by creating value, not by taking control away from the person who owns the computer.»
